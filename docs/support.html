Answers to common questions about the document scanner & OCR app for iPhone.

Frequently asked

How do I scan a document?

Tap the scan button on the Documents screen and point your camera at a page. Pagewise detects the edges automatically and captures a corrected, cropped image.

Is scanning free?

Yes. Capturing, tagging, favoriting, and browsing your documents is free with no limit. Pagewise Pro is only required to export or share a finished document as a PDF or text file.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your own iCloud account if you're signed in. Pagewise does not run its own servers and never receives your documents.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Pagewise Pro to cancel. Changes take effect at the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Pagewise, go to About, tap Upgrade to Pro, then Restore Purchases.
